The 7-Day Mission Schedule

Experience a week of transformation and impact

Days 1-2

Launch & Community Engagement

Orientation, team building, and one-on-one evangelism in local communities. Building relationships and understanding needs.

Day 3

Impact Day

Mass evangelism events with live music and broadcast ministry. Philanthropic distribution to prisoners, orphans, widows, PWDs, and refugees.

Days 4-5

Leadership & Biblical Education

Leadership/Workers Conferences sharing best practices. Minister's Bible Study focused on service-oriented scripture and kingdom principles.

Days 6-7

Family Outreach & Reflection

Family-focused ministry, volunteer appreciation, reflection sessions, and commitment ceremonies for new believers and partners.
