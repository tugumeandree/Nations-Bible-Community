📜 Scripture Safaris

Biblical Heritage & Spiritual Enrichment

Experience the Bible come alive as you explore sacred sites, deepen your understanding of Scripture, and walk in the footsteps of biblical history.

Walk Where Jesus Walked

A Scripture Safari is more than a tour—it's a transformative spiritual journey. We combine historical exploration with in-depth Bible study, bringing the Scriptures to life in the very places where biblical events unfolded.

Two Annual Tours

  • One regional tour to local biblical heritage sites
  • One international tour to Israel and Middle Eastern biblical locations
  • Limited group sizes for intimate, meaningful experiences
  • Expert guides and biblical scholars

What Makes Us Different

  • Interactive learning, not just sightseeing
  • Daily Bible studies at relevant locations
  • Cultural immersion and historical context
  • Spiritual reflection and personal growth

Sacred Sites We Explore

🕌

Jerusalem

The City of David

Walk the ancient streets, visit the Temple Mount, pray at the Western Wall, and experience the Garden of Gethsemane. Explore the City of David and understand Jesus's final week.

⛪

Capernaum

Jesus's Hometown

Stand in the synagogue where Jesus taught, see Peter's house, and study the Sermon on the Mount at the Mount of Beatitudes overlooking the Sea of Galilee.

⛰️

Mount Sinai

Where God Gave the Law

Trek to the summit where Moses received the Ten Commandments. Experience sunrise worship and study the Exodus narrative in its historical setting.

🏛️

Bethlehem

Birthplace of the Messiah

Visit the Church of the Nativity, explore the shepherds' fields, and study the prophecies of Christ's birth in the very place they were fulfilled.

🌊

Jordan River

Site of Baptism

Stand at the traditional baptism site of Jesus, reflect on the ministry of John the Baptist, and optionally participate in baptism renewal ceremonies.

🏜️

Qumran

Dead Sea Scrolls Discovery

Explore the caves where ancient biblical manuscripts were found. Learn about the preservation of Scripture and the reliability of God's Word.

🌳

Garden Tomb

Resurrection Site

Visit the traditional site of Jesus's burial and resurrection. Experience communion and worship at this sacred location.

🛶

Sea of Galilee

Where Jesus Walked on Water

Boat ride on the sea, visit fishing villages, study Jesus's miracles, and reflect on His teachings by the shore.

Sacred Journeys

International Biblical Tours

Journey to the Holy Land and beyond. Experience Israel, Egypt, Jordan, Greece, and Turkey—walking in the footsteps of Jesus, the apostles, and Old Testament heroes.

  • Comprehensive 10-14 day itineraries
  • Expert biblical guides and teachers
  • Comfortable accommodations
  • All-inclusive packages available

Sample 10-Day Holy Land Safari

Day 1

Arrival in Tel Aviv → Jerusalem

Welcome dinner, orientation, and prayer at the Mount of Olives overlooking Jerusalem.

Day 2

Old City of Jerusalem

Western Wall, Temple Mount, Via Dolorosa, Church of the Holy Sepulchre. Evening: Worship and communion.

Day 3

Bethlehem & Shepherd's Fields

Church of the Nativity, study of Christmas narrative, visit to refugee camp ministry.

Day 4

Dead Sea & Masada

Qumran caves, Masada fortress, float in the Dead Sea, study of Scripture preservation.

Day 5

Journey to Galilee

Jordan River baptism site, Jericho, travel north through the Jordan Valley.

Day 6

Sea of Galilee

Boat ride, Capernaum synagogue, Mount of Beatitudes, study of Jesus's Galilean ministry.

Day 7

Nazareth & Mount Tabor

Jesus's hometown, synagogue where He read Isaiah, traditional site of Transfiguration.

Day 8

Caesarea & Haifa

Roman theater where Peter preached, aqueducts, Mount Carmel (Elijah's victory), Bahai gardens.

Day 9

Return to Jerusalem

City of David, Hezekiah's Tunnel, Garden Tomb, final worship and reflection session.

Day 10

Departure

Final breakfast, group photo, journey home with transformed hearts and renewed faith.
