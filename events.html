Events & Schedule

Weekly Bible Study

Every Friday · 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Join us every Friday evening for in-depth Bible study, fellowship, and spiritual growth. Our gatherings are designed to help you grow deeper in God's Word and connect with fellow believers.

📍 In Person: Media Studio at Semambo Close, Najjera, Kampala

💻 Live Online: Facebook, YouTube, Instagram

🎥 Video Call: Zoom/Google Meet (links shared via WhatsApp community)

Special First Friday Events

Monthly Worship Night

First Friday of Every Month · 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM

A special evening dedicated to worship, praise, and seeking God's presence together through music and prayer.

Vision Night

First Friday of January · 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Start the year with vision! Join us for a powerful night of prophetic declaration, setting spiritual goals, and aligning with God's purposes for the new year.

Hymns & Dinner

First Friday of June & December · 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Celebrate with traditional hymns of faith and share a communal dinner together. A special time of fellowship, thanksgiving, and remembering God's faithfulness.

Regular Bible Study

All Other Fridays · 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Verse-by-verse study with discussion, prayer, and practical application. All are welcome to join us in growing deeper in Scripture.
