*"And now, Lord, behold their threatenings: and grant unto thy servants, that with all boldness they may speak thy word, By stretching forth thine hand to heal; and that signs and wonders may be done by the name of thy holy child Jesus." (Acts 4:29-30)*

The Holy Spirit is the greatest teacher and guide one could ever have. His primary instrument of transformation is the Word of God. 

Of all the treasures the Spirit of God imparts to your spirit, nothing surpasses the Word. 

The Word is the "core product" of the Kingdom; it is the material of which we are built and the food that sustains our divine nature.

Furthermore, the Spirit of God desires a continuous, unbroken fellowship with you through prayer. This is why the Apostles declared in Acts 6:4, *"But we will give ourselves continually to prayer, and to the ministry of the word."* 

This is the foundation of a successful Christian life.

However, your devotion to the Word and prayer must not be an end in itself; it must produce results in the earth! 

**This year, you must purposefully make notable miracles and soul-winning (conversions) your target.**

The early Church didn't just study the Word in a vacuum; they had a goal for impact.

## The Brand of the Almighty

Why are miracles so vital? It is because God has a "brand" to maintain as the Omnipotent One. 

He is the God of the impossible! From generation to generation, He demonstrates His power to prove His existence and His love. 

When you work miracles, you are advertising the character of the Father.

## The Expansion of the Kingdom

Conversions are the lifeblood of the Kingdom. Think of it this way:

* The Word is the core product.
* Miracles and love are the "branding and marketing" that arrest the attention of the world.
* Salvation is the "lead generation" that brings men into the fold.
* The Church is the "community engagement" where they are nurtured.

God's Kingdom advances through faith in the finished work of Christ. That is how new members "subscribe" to the life of glory. 

As you go out this year, don't just speak words; expect the supernatural! 

Let there be such a demonstration of the Spirit that men will have no choice but to bow to the Lordship of Jesus. Hallelujah!

## CONFESSION

Dear Father, I thank you for the power of your Word and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit. I am a co-laborer with you, and this year, I set my heart on notable miracles and massive conversions. I refuse to have a form of godliness without the power. As I preach the Gospel, you are confirming your Word with signs following, and many are being added to the Kingdom through me, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
