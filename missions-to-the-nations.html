🌍 Missions to the Nations

Active Outreach & Transformation

Two annual 7-day intensive missions bringing hope, healing, and transformation through evangelism, philanthropy, leadership training, and biblical education.

The 7-Day Mission Experience

A comprehensive journey of transformation

Day 1

Launch & Orientation

Arrival, team building exercises, mission briefing, prayer and preparation. Setting the spiritual tone for the week ahead.

Day 2

Community Engagement

One-on-one evangelism in neighborhoods, building relationships, understanding community needs, door-to-door ministry.

Day 3

Impact Day

Mass Evangelism: Large-scale crusade with live music, testimonies, and altar calls. Philanthropic Distribution: Gifts and supplies to prisoners, orphans, widows, PWDs, and refugees.

Day 4

Leadership Conference

Annual gathering of ministry leaders and workers. Sharing best practices, vision casting, strategic planning, and kingdom collaboration.

Day 5

Minister's Bible Study

Intensive biblical education focused on service, ministry excellence, and kingdom theology. Interactive teaching and practical application.

Day 6

Family Outreach

Ministry to families and children, volunteer appreciation ceremonies, testimonies from the week, community meals and fellowship.

Day 7

Reflection & Commitment

Commitment ceremonies for new believers, baptisms, follow-up planning, team debriefing, celebration of God's faithfulness.
